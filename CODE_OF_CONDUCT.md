# EnoughFactory community conduct

We want people to contribute, ask questions and disagree without being harassed or excluded. Treat other contributors with respect and discuss the work in concrete terms.

Be considerate of different experience levels, backgrounds and communication styles. Give actionable feedback. Respect privacy and consent; never publish another person's private messages, credentials or personal information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexual attention without consent, deliberate disruption and retaliation against someone who raises a concern are unacceptable in project discussions and community spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, limit participation or exclude participants when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate and explained without exposing private information. Maintainers are expected to follow the same standards.

To raise a concern, use GitHub's reporting controls for the relevant content. For concerns requiring private technical disclosure, use the reporting path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). A public discussion is appropriate only when it can be handled without sharing private information.
